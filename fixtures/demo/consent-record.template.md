## Special cases

- **Minors:** a parent/guardian must consent; do not film identifiable minors
  otherwise.
- **Bystanders:** redact faces/plates unless they have their own consent.
- **Private property:** the owner must permit filming.
- **Repairer:** disclose whether they are a real contractor, volunteer or staged
  actor.
- **Inspector:** disclose whether the inspection is independent or a
  demonstration.
