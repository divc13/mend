MEND — Maintenance with memory — is a community asset repair protocol on NEAR. A broken bench, rack, fence or community-centre door moves through one permanent lifecycle: walletless report, private triage, steward scope, Sputnik DAO authorization, sealed contractor bids, deterministic hash-pinned scoring, physical repair, independent human inspection, 90% completion payment and a 10% durability holdback released only after a later inspection. If durability fails, MEND reopens the same work order and warranty history instead of creating a new bounty. Raw media and precise locations stay off-chain; the browser strips EXIF/GPS before upload and only redacted hashes and public state go on-chain. AI suggests duplicates and scope but never approves, awards or pays. A full API-backed live app runs at https://mend-demo.vercel.app with Vercel Functions and Neon Postgres. A real NEAR testnet lifecycle (`WO-BENCH-V2-001-0002`, 42 transactions) reached CLOSED with exact 100/20/72/8 test-mUSDC accounting and escrow 0. Live NEAR AI signature, Sputnik DAO, OutLayer, NEP-366 and Agent Market evidence exists; the Intents deposit/settlement, public repository, X post and real repair media remain human-gated. No city adoption or partnership is claimed.
