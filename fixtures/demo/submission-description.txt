MEND — Maintenance with memory — is a community asset repair protocol on NEAR. A broken bench, rack, fence or community-centre door moves through one permanent lifecycle: walletless report, private triage, steward scope, Sputnik DAO authorization, cross-chain sponsorship via NEAR Intents, sealed contractor bids, deterministic hash-pinned bid scoring on OutLayer, physical repair, independent human inspection, 90% completion payment, and a 10% durability holdback released only after a later inspection. If durability fails, MEND reopens the same work order and warranty history instead of creating a new bounty. Raw media and precise locations stay off-chain; only redacted hashes and public state go on-chain. AI suggests but never approves, awards or pays. The repository is in active integration: the protocol and 100/80/72/8 accounting are tested locally; live testnet, hosted demo, demo video and physical repair evidence remain blocked on human-supplied credentials, funds, consent and approvals. No city adoption or partnership is claimed.
